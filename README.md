### 🥉 IZee Job Fair — End-to-End Placement Event Operations *(private repo)*

Not a registration form — a full event-operations system spanning **candidate registration → digital pass generation → event-day validation → attendance**.

- **Two registration channels:** pre-registration + fast on-the-spot walk-in flow with auto-approval
- **Three operational roles:** admin (management/exports), volunteer (QR/SID validation), candidate (registration/pass)
- **Digital QR passes:** zero-storage pass generation with automated and bulk "Resend All" email delivery via Brevo
- **Event-day validation:** camera QR scanning with manual SID fallback
- **Live operations:** handled **500+ attendees**, supported **50+ volunteers** and **50+ recruiting companies**
- Designed for high-volume job-fair operations rather than simple form submission

`React 19` `FastAPI` `Supabase` `JWT` `Pillow` `Brevo` `Railway`

### IZee Got Talent — Multi-Role Fest Registration & Entry System *(private repo)*

A cultural-fest platform designed around **two fundamentally different audiences — general attendees and competition participants — rather than a single registration flow.**

- **Audience:** general fest attendees with a standard registration and approval workflow
- **Participants:** competition entrants who can select a **maximum of 2 competitions**
- **Pending-first approval:** `Registration → Faculty Review → Approval → QR Generation → Entry`
- **Faculty operations:** search, filtering by course/year/event, sorting, pagination, and CSV export
- **Event-day validation:** camera QR scanning + manual pass-ID fallback
- **Live event operations:** managed **350+ attendees** with real-time approval and entry tracking

`React` `FastAPI` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `SMTP`

### Zerve-Pulse — Behavioral Retention & Power-User Prediction Engine *(hosted on Zerve, not GitHub)*
Built at **Zerve × HackerEarth** — predicts which users will become long-term "power users" from behavioral telemetry alone, not credits or signups.
- Defined success as the top 20% by engagement (crossing the 80th percentile on interaction volume), then engineered 3 behavioral features — `total_events`, `unique_events`, `session_count` — that together explain **84.5%** of what predicts retention
- Handled class imbalance with a custom k-NN SMOTE implementation (no external library) — 2,586 synthetic samples
- Calibrated the decision threshold to 0.10 (vs. default 0.50) to catch users during early discovery, deliberately trading some precision for **100% recall** on high-potential users — **0 missed power users** across 5,410 total users
- RandomForest (300 trees) → **99.09% precision, 99.82% accuracy**
- Shipped as a live **3-tier REST API** (Power / Growing / Dormant) for real-time tier assignment

`Python` `scikit-learn` `RandomForest` `Custom k-NN SMOTE` `REST API`
